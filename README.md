Ludo Nova 0.1.2 — early access

- Centered home circles and tokens on one shared board grid.
- Ad-free practice, pass-and-play, online rooms, teams and tournaments.
- Persistent wallet, inventory, original boards, dice and token styles.
- Room messages, optional voice, player reports and blocking.
- Fixed team forfeits so a departed partner cannot strand a match.
- Added receipt verification and refund reconciliation safeguards.

Paid checkout is awaiting Google Play setup and testing. Shop prices currently show the planned South African prices at one fifth of the observed reference amounts, rounded to cents. Earned game currency can already unlock cosmetics. Online play requires an internet connection. Voice connects directly between players when their networks allow it.
